— Who We Are

Built by Traders,
for Future Traders

MarketMind Academy was founded by a team of SEBI-certified analysts, fund managers, and active traders who saw a persistent gap: retail investors had passion but no structured learning path.

We've compressed years of market experience into practical, project-based programs tailored to Indian markets — NSE, BSE, NIFTY, and SENSEX. Whether you're a salaried professional, college student, or aspiring full-time trader, we meet you where you are.

Our curriculum is rooted in real trades, real charts, and real decisions — not textbook theory or generic finance content.
